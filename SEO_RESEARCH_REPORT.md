# Search and AI visibility research — Abdullah Sher Muhammad

Research date: 25 September 2026. Site: https://abdullahshermuhammad.me/. Scope: all 23 public pages in the static export. Primary offer: practical AI automation and connected business workflows designed by Abdullah Sher Muhammad, founder of Devisgon.

## Method and limits

This is a **qualitative search-intent and page-mapping study**, not a paid keyword-database export or a ranking report. I reviewed the site’s actual copy and routes, sampled live search results for broad service, home-service and international-market phrases, and checked current guidance from Google, Bing, OpenAI and Anthropic. The sampled results reveal competing page types and wording; they do **not** establish monthly search volume, keyword difficulty, or local rank. I had no access to this domain’s Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, Google Ads Keyword Planner, site analytics, or actual AI referral logs. No positions, traffic estimates or future AI recommendations are claimed.

The dominant commercial intent is **a service provider who can connect calls, leads, CRM and existing tools into a workable process**. Broad “AI automation agency” results are crowded; specific workflow and trade queries are better initial landing-page targets. Representative observed examples include [HVAC AI receptionist pages](https://targetdial.co/solutions/hvac), [roofing follow-up systems](https://www.invowork.com/roofing), [Dubai automation services](https://cressoftmarketing.ae/services/ai-automation), [Saudi AI receptionists](https://www.imqusai.com/), and [Qatar WhatsApp chatbots](https://omnifai.qa/). These examples show query intent and competition, not an endorsement or a measured ranking forecast.

Country and city examples describe **where a matching query could originate**, not areas where the domain currently ranks. Search Console country/query/page data and Bing Webmaster data are needed to report real visibility. Google advises locale-specific URLs and hreflang when genuine language/region variants exist; targeting one country can reduce relevance elsewhere. City-variant pages with substantially duplicated text and no distinct local value risk doorway abuse. See [Google international guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites) and [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

## Changes implemented

1. Rewrote the homepage title and description to describe a real AI automation consultant for service businesses, and aligned the hero paragraph with the same offer.
2. Gave all 23 pages distinct intent-specific titles and descriptions. Seven service pages and four industry pages use a shared typed keyword map in `src/content/seo-targets.ts`, while each page retains its own canonical URL and Open Graph/X preview.
3. Added a unique, visible explanation and practical question/answer to each of those 11 specialist pages. Claims describe possible implementations conditionally and avoid fabricated deployments or performance metrics.
4. Added a generated `sitemap.xml` for all 23 canonical public pages and a `robots.txt` that allows crawling and declares the sitemap. The existing OG image is referenced at `/images/og-image.png`.
5. Added truthful homepage `Person` and `WebSite` JSON-LD: Abdullah’s name, profession, real Okara location, Devisgon association and public LinkedIn profile. The About page states the same location visibly. There is no fake rating, review, local office or unsupported FAQ rich-result claim.

Google says meaningful page titles/headings and helpful content matter, and AI Overviews/AI Mode need the same foundational SEO and indexed pages with eligible snippets; special “AI SEO” markup is not required. See [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials), [AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) and [sitemap instructions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap).
